Our Story

Founded in 2020, Urban Nest began with a simple mission: to make modern living accessible to everyone. We believe that your home should be a reflection of your personality - a sanctuary where style meets comfort.

What started as a small furniture store has grown into a comprehensive home & living destination, offering everything from statement furniture pieces to everyday essentials. We curate products that combine functionality with aesthetic appeal, ensuring every item tells a story.

Today, we serve thousands of happy customers across the country, helping them create spaces they love to call home.
